## About

I'm a Computer Science student at East West University in Dhaka and a research
assistant at the DHMAINetRG group, where I work on computer networks and IoT
protocols. Much of my research uses the ONE Simulator to study how
Internet-of-Vehicles traffic behaves on the real road network of Dhaka — work
that has so far produced a few IEEE conference papers. Alongside research, I
enjoy building software, from a patient-owned health records platform to a
metro ticketing CLI in C.

I'm still early in my research journey, and I learn something new on every
project. Happy to talk about networking, simulation, or software.

## Research Interests

- **Internet-of-Vehicles (IoV)** — realistic urban mobility and DTN routing
- **IoT protocols** — context-aware, energy-conscious reliability at the edge
- **Network simulation** — reproducible experiments with the ONE Simulator
